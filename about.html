About Family Paws

We’re here to make the journey to a loving home a little easier—for pets and people.

Why we’re here

Love makes a family.

Family Paws is a student-created pet adoption website with one simple idea: every animal deserves care, comfort, and a chance to belong.

We share pet profiles and encourage visitors to learn about adoption before welcoming a companion home.

💚

Our mission

Connect adorable pets with caring families and promote responsible adoption.

🌼

Our vision

A kinder world where every pet is safe, loved, and treated with respect.

🐾

Our values

Compassion, patience, honesty, and lifelong commitment to animal care.

Our furry inspiration

So many reasons to smile

Before adopting, remember…

  • Pets need time, attention, and patience.
  • Plan for food, grooming, and veterinary care.
  • Choose a pet that fits your home and lifestyle.
